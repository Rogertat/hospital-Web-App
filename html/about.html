About Us

Founded in 2001, XeraCare Hospital grew from a single cardiology clinic into a network of six care centers with twelve departments. Our promise has stayed the same: consultant-led care, plain-language answers, and appointments that respect your time.

  • Consultant-led, always

    Every first visit is with a consultant — never a queue, never a trainee-only appointment

  • Plain-language results

    Findings explained on the day, with a written summary within two working days

  • Privacy by design

    Our online forms never ask for medical details — clinical information stays in the consulting room

A note on this site: XeraCare Hospital is a fictional brand created to demonstrate a web analytics pipeline. No real medical services are offered, and no real patient data is collected.

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers about visiting XeraCare

What are the visiting hours?

Our care centers are open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, with evening consultation slots on Tuesdays and Thursdays until 9:30 PM.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the online booking form — choose a service, pick a preferred date and time, and our scheduling team confirms by email, usually within one working day.

Is parking available?

Yes — every XeraCare center has free patient parking, with accessible spaces next to the main entrance.

Do you work with insurance providers?

Our billing team can prepare itemized invoices suitable for most insurers. Bring your policy details to your first visit and we will handle the paperwork with you.

Is this a real hospital?

No. XeraCare Hospital is a fictional demonstration site used to showcase a web analytics pipeline. No real medical services are offered.
